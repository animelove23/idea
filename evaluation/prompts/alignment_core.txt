Decision order:
1. Identify the fact's subject/event and information dimension. Compare FULL original and steer captions, not merely missing entries in their fact lists.
2. retained: the complete proposition is equivalent, including participants, modality, polarity, quantity scope and units. Synonyms and reordered mentions are retained. Duplicate copies of the same complete proposition may be grouped as one primary edge. Do not group independent information or differently qualified statements as duplicates.
3. modified: both sides explicitly describe the SAME confidently corresponding subject/group/event and the SAME information dimension, but change its value, specificity, event phase, qualifier, or relation target. It is a semantic description change, not necessarily a real-world contradiction. Examples of dimensions: entity designation; color; count of a fixed population; body posture; stage of the same activity; a fixed subject's above relation. More-specific/general entity descriptions can be modified when contextual identity is secure and their meanings are not equivalent. A stage or target change does NOT by itself make identity uncertain. Preparing an action versus performing that same action can be modified under this protocol. Use consistent binding slot names for the shared dimension even when wording differs, e.g. event_activity or above. A same-subject above relation can change from target A to target B if both targets are identified. Each modified primary edge must have exactly ONE fact per side. No forced pairing based merely on common nouns or verbs.
4. removed/added: a complete proposition is no longer/newly expressed in the opposite FULL caption. If a red object becomes an object with no color mentioned, its existence is retained and its separate red-color fact is removed, not modified. If the source says nothing about footwear and the target adds footwear, that is added, even if its wearer cannot be identified among two people. Different dimensions are removed plus added when absence/newness is clear (material vs color, age vs color, action vs independent location). Same subject does not justify pairing all its properties as modified. Likewise, a total population count versus a count of a specific activity subgroup is not a same-population count change: evaluate them separately for added/removed using both full captions. A total spanning two activities does not entail a specified count in one activity. Vague quantity changes (several/few, exact/vague, multiple/several) for the SAME population can be modified; do not invent numerical equivalents. Pure singular/plural existence is not automatically an exact count.
5. other: comparison cannot be established reliably: unresolved instance identity needed for this particular fact; extraction_gap; incompatible bundled granularity; unreliable source; or out-of-scope subjective content. Do not put a clearly identified change here simply because it is partial overlap or a generalization. Do not use other to avoid a difficult but explicit added/removed decision. Conversely, do not equate differences in independent axes (gaze direction vs target distance) simply because the subject and action verb match. Preserve all other facts with a reason for audit; they will be excluded from substantive change counts. Never disguise an extraction failure as removal. If the opposite caption still states a fact absent from its extraction, other/extraction_gap requires an exact quote from the opposite caption. Fact type other always receives status other/other_scope.
