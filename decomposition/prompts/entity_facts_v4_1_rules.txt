The ONLY five shapes (plus the common fields above):
1. object: subject=<entity ID>, predicate=exists. Existence only, no object/value/target. Explicitly described visual groups/classes and specific entities can coexist as object claims; do not mechanically create objects for every noun or abstract reference anchor. Speculatively introduced entities can have speculative existence claims; do not delete them because they may be hallucinated.
2. attribute: subject=<entity ID>, predicate=<one property>, value=<nonempty string or finite number>. No object/target. Color, size, material, appearance, position-in-image and other explicitly stated properties. Separate independently changeable colors/properties. Do not hide a relationship to another physical entity inside a value.
3. action: subject=<entity ID>, predicate=<action/phase/posture>, optional object=<entity ID>. No value/target. Keep try_to_catch, be_ready_to_throw, prepare_to_serve, will_use_for_cutting distinct from actual catch/throw/serve/cut. Holding/wearing belong to relation. Do not make static placement (is placed/positioned) into a second redundant action.
4. relation: subject=<entity ID>, predicate=<relation>, object=<entity ID>. BOTH ends are required. Examples: holds, wears, on, near, next_to, part_of (only explicitly grounded). 'man sitting on mattress on floor' gives sit(man), on(man,mattress), on(mattress,floor); NO inferred on(man,floor). Do not propagate location through contains, ownership, group membership or common sense. Directly stated apparently inconsistent locations are still retained.
5. count: target=<canonical class string OR list of existing entity IDs>, predicate=<count comparator>, value=<number or original vague quantity>. NO subject/object. Explicit two -> count=2; at least two -> count_gte=2; more than -> count_gt; at most -> count_lte; fewer than -> count_lt. count_eq also means exact. Explicit one/another/third can ground a lower bound on the CATEGORY; do not invent counts merely from the number of IDs. Preserve exact versus lower-bound scope and counting units.
