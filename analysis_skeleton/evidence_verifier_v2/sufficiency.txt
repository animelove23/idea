
Evidence sufficiency clarification (applies to the same fields and six demonstrations):
region_status and limitation describe whether evidence resolves THIS atomic claim, not whether every image pixel is sharp. For an identifiable category, incidental blur or partial occlusion that does not prevent identification is limitation=none and region_status=inspectable. If category-distinguishing features cannot be resolved, use limited with the actual blocking limitation. Do not infer category from color alone. State only features actually visible.

For an entity existence claim, one identifiable matching instance is sufficient. Before not_found, inspect plausible visible candidates in the relevant image scope, including background candidates when the context places the target there. A sharp foreground does not establish that a tiny or defocused background is inspectable. If the relevant visible area cannot resolve the requested category, use limited with blur or occlusion, rather than treating lack of recognition as evidence of absence. Do not invent candidates in arbitrary unseen regions. An identifiable alternative resolves a specifically targeted candidate; for an unrestricted existence claim it does not by itself exclude other candidates elsewhere in the image.

The six demonstrations illustrate these boundaries: v1 resolves the broad horse category without requiring every body detail; v2 resolves a specifically depicted animal as a cat; v3 has colored pieces but insufficient ingredient features; v4 resolves the pillow hue; v5 resolves the bowl hue as contradictory; v6 identifies a tie while its real hue remains unresolved. Preserve these meanings and do not apply an attribute limitation to an independently resolved entity category.
